Solutions

Four ways we can fight malnutrition.

Healthy Eating

Eat fruits, vegetables, proteins and whole grains.

Try it: add sukuma wiki, beans or a fruit to one meal every day.

Nutrition Education

Teach families about balanced diets.

Try it: share what you learned about food groups with your family.

Food Support

Provide meals to vulnerable communities.

Try it: school meal programmes help children eat well and learn better.

Healthcare

Regular health check-ups help detect malnutrition early.

Try it: clinics measure a child's height and weight to check their growth.

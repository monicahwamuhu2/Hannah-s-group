Poor Nutrition and Malnutrition

What causes it, and what it does to people.

The problem in numbers

150 million

children under 5 are too short for their age (stunting)

43 million

children under 5 are too thin for their height (wasting)

36 million

children under 5 are too heavy for their height (overweight)

Source: World Health Organization (WHO), figures for 2024.

Causes

  • Hunger caused by poverty
  • Lack of balanced diets
  • Vitamin and mineral deficiencies
  • Eating too much junk food and sugar

Effects

  • Stunted growth in children
  • Weak immune systems
  • Poor academic performance
  • Higher risk of diseases

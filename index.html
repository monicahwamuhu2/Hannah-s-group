Good Health and Well-being

Our project focuses on poor nutrition and malnutrition.

Goal of SDG 3

Sustainable Development Goal 3 aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for everyone at every age.

One of the biggest health challenges today is poor nutrition and malnutrition, which affect millions of children and adults worldwide.

Food and health are closely linked. When children do not get enough healthy food, they get sick more easily. Around 45% of deaths of children under five are linked to undernutrition.

Source: World Health Organization (WHO), Child growth.
